## Description
The first lines show before "more", so they carry the pitch and say "Washington State" right away.

```
Washington State's restaurants, and the teriyaki Seattle made its own.

Washington Eats is a free guide to eating in Washington State: 179 teriyaki shops, 51 phở shops, 76 drive-ins and 91 oyster bars and seafood places we checked by hand, plus 20,247 restaurants, cafés, espresso stands and bars in 406 towns, from Seattle and Spokane to Tacoma, Vancouver, the Tri-Cities, Yakima, Bellingham and Olympia. No account, no ads.

WASHINGTON GUIDES
• Seattle Teriyaki: teriyaki counters statewide, with what each one serves. Seattle-style teriyaki is traced to Toshi Kasahara's shop of 1976; the app says which Toshi's is his.
• Phở: phở shops from Little Saigon to Spokane.
• Drive-In Burgers: Dick's, Zip's, Burgerville, Burgermaster and small-town burger stands.
• Oysters & Seafood: oyster bars, chowder and fish and chips, from Puget Sound to Willapa Bay.
• Honors & History: James Beard finalists and semifinalists, and long-running institutions.
• Oldest Places: opening years we could verify, oldest first.
• Food Safety: King County's official food safety ratings (Excellent, Good, Okay, Needs to Improve), shown as Public Health – Seattle & King County publishes them.

FIND IT FAST
• Sort by distance, or browse the map by teriyaki, phở, drive-ins or seafood.
• Search by name, town, street or dish: "spicy chicken kent", "pho spokane".
• Filter by town or cuisine, hide chains or espresso stands.
• Save places, and find hand-checked places from iPhone search (Spotlight).

LIVE DETAILS FROM APPLE MAPS
Tap "Ratings, hours & photos" on any place to open Apple Maps' own place card, with current hours, photos, ratings and directions.

HOW THE LISTS ARE BUILT
Every teriyaki shop, phở shop, drive-in and seafood place was checked by hand in October 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: its own website, menu or ordering page, or dated local news. The rest of the restaurants come from Overture Maps open data and King County's public inspection records, checked for reliability against those records. Website links are checked too. Nothing is ranked by star ratings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location, if you share it, only sorts lists on your device. Saved places stay on your device.

Places open and close, so check before you go. Spot a mistake? Email work-with-nick@gmail.com.

An independent app, not affiliated with any restaurant, team, chain or government agency (including King County and Public Health – Seattle & King County), or with the James Beard Foundation. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Place data from Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA Permissive 2.0) and others; sources and licenses are on the About tab.
```
