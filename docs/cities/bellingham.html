    424 W Bakerview Rd #101, Bellingham

    Teriyaki Serves: chicken teriyaki; pork teriyaki; beef teriyaki; chicken teriyaki bowl; katsu; yakisoba; gyoza; sushi rolls · Business founded 1993

  2. Super Duper Teriyaki

    2019 Harris Ave, Bellingham

    Teriyaki Serves: chicken teriyaki; spicy chicken teriyaki; beef teriyaki; pork teriyaki; kalbi short rib; mochiko chicken; spam teriyaki; chicken katsu; yakisoba; gyoza

Oysters and seafood near Bellingham

Shellfish and seafood counters within 12 miles of Bellingham. Statewide: 91 hand-checked oyster bars and seafood places.

  1. Anthony's

    25 Bellwether Way, Bellingham

    Seafood Serves: pan-fried Willapa Bay oysters; New England-style clam chowder; Dungeness crab; wild salmon; halibut; wild Alaska true cod 'n chips · Business founded 1973

    Anthony's seafood restaurant on Squalicum Harbor, with brunch on Sundays. The Anthony's group was founded in Bellevue in 1973.

    anthonys.com

  2. Port Fairhaven Fish & Chips

    355 Harris Ave (Bellingham Cruise Terminal), Bellingham

    Seafood Serves: cod basket; halibut basket; salmon basket; clam strips basket; clam chowder; whiskey crab soup

    Fish-and-chips counter inside the Bellingham Cruise Terminal in Fairhaven, with whiskey crab soup. Open daily.

    fairhavenfish.com

  3. Rock and Rye Oyster House

    1145 N State St, Bellingham

    Seafood Serves: oysters on the half shell (Massimo from Samish Bay, Judd Cove from Orcas Island); oysters Rockefeller; pan-fried oysters; oyster shooter; beer-battered cod fish & chips; saffron corn chowder with clams; seared halibut · Since 2014

    Downtown Bellingham oyster house, established in 2014, with a rotating list of Washington oysters. Dinner Tuesday to Sunday.

  4. Waterfront Tavern Seafood and Bar Bellingham

    521 W Holly St, Bellingham

    Seafood Serves: beer-battered Pacific cod fish & chips; clam chowder; clam strips; fresh oysters · Since 1910

    Dive bar with a seafood kitchen over the mouth of Whatcom Creek, at this spot since 1910. Also serves breakfast, with pool and darts.

    waterfronttavernseafoodbar.com

  5. Chuckanut Manor Seafood & Grill

    3056 Chuckanut Dr, Bow · 11 mi from Bellingham

    Seafood Serves: oysters Rockefeller (Pacific oysters); fried oysters; Taylor Shellfish clams; clam chowder; Alaskan halibut & chips; Northwest cioppino; pistachio-crusted rockfish

    Seafood and grill restaurant on Chuckanut Drive overlooking Samish Bay, in a building that dates to the 1930s and was expanded in 1968.

    chuckanutmanor.com
