Chains and independents

The state's regional drive-in brands are on the list location by location: Zip's Drive-In (15), Burgerville (12), Dick's Drive-In (9), Burgermaster (4), Kidd Valley (3). Each brand's founding year comes from its own site. The other 26 are independent stands, many in small towns, some seasonal; when a place says it closes for winter, its note says so.

How the list was built: every location was checked against a 2025 or 2026 source, such as the brand's own location list or the stand's own menu. Places in King County also show King County's official food safety rating, as published. The order is by region and town, never by rating.
