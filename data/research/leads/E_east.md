# Eastern and central Washington teriyaki: unverified leads (checked 2026-10-05)

## Candidates I couldn't verify
- Original J's Teriyaki, 140 Eastmont Ave, East Wenatchee: the listed domain originaljsteriyaki.com doesn't resolve. The only 2025 mention is a KPQ listicle (Sept 11, 2025) built from Yelp quotes, which doesn't count. Third-party ordering sites (Dashing Drivers) and a Visit Wenatchee directory entry exist. Needs its own menu/ordering page or a news story.
- Mr. Teriyaki, 805 N Main St, Ellensburg: no website. Only DoorDash, Yelp and directory pages found. Needs its own site or a news source.
- Teriyaki Wok (also "Teriyaki & Wok"), 1210 S Canyon Rd, Ellensburg: the listed teriyakiwok.placeweb.site now redirects to a parked-domain page. Only aggregators and Facebook found.
- Osaka Teriyaki and Sushi, 4101 W 27th Pl, Kennewick: the listed "website" is a cityseekr directory page. Only Uber Eats, DoorDash and aggregators found.
- Tokyo Sushi & Teriyaki, 3617 Plaza Way #B, Kennewick: only a Google business.site page (not acceptable) and a 509-local directory.
- New York Teriyaki Moxee, 214 E Moxee Ave, Moxee: the listed website is Uber Eats. Teriyaki Express Wapato's site has a gift-card link named "NEWYORKTERIYAKIMOXEE", which suggests the two are connected, but nothing confirms it. Needs its own page or news.
- New York Teriyaki, 3710 Fruitvale Blvd, Yakima: no own site found (only a hub.biz menu page and Yellow Pages). The other four New York Teriyaki shops (Union Gap, 72nd Ave, N 1st St, Selah) were verified.
- Golden Teriyaki, 1285 N Grand Ave, Pullman: the listed site is the pullmanmenus aggregator. Aggregators describe it as a Chinese restaurant. goldenteriyaki.com is an unrelated restaurant in Long Beach, CA.
- Oasis Teriyaki And Pho, 530 E Main St, Pullman: no website. Tripadvisor marks it closed (not an acceptable source). Probably closed; confirm before moving it to the closed file.
- Bento Teriyaki, 61 Columbia Point Dr, Richland: the listed "website" is the Tri-City Herald calendar. Nothing else found.
- Sake Express Sushi & Teriyaki, 2576 Queensgate Dr, Richland: the listed domain sakeexpresssushi.com now redirects to an unrelated Atlanta nail-salon site, so it looks hijacked; don't use it. A BentoBox site (sakeexpresssushiteriyaki.getbento.com) is live with hours but no menu, and its ordering portal is deactivated. Needs a menu or a 2025-26 news source.
- Shogun Teriyaki & Sushi, 760 Dalton St, Richland: only Facebook and DoorDash/Postmates found. shogunteriyaki.com is a GoDaddy "launching soon" page ((c) 2025) with no location, so I can't tie it to Richland.
- Le's Teriyaki, 2018 N Hamilton St, Spokane: the listed webs.com site doesn't resolve. No other source.
- Midori Sushi and Teriyaki, 927 W Garland Ave, Spokane: the listed domain kimsteriyakispokane.com doesn't resolve (possibly a former Kim's Teriyaki). Only Nextdoor, Yelp and aggregators. Also likely sushi-led; check the menu.
- Sakura Teriyaki & Sushi, 2000 Yakima Valley Hwy, Sunnyside: the listed edan.io site returned 403, so I skipped it.
- Wok Teriyaki, 1601 E Washington Ave Ste 103, Union Gap: only a 2014 Yakima Herald "On the Menu" piece (updated 2022). Needs a 2025-26 source.
- Tri Teriyaki, 5431 W Van Giesen St, West Richland: its own Blogger site was last updated in 2013. Facebook only. Needs a current source.
- Famous Burger and Teriyaki Sandwich, 401 W Lincoln Ave, Yakima: the listed domain famousburgeryakima.com serves a redirect/ad-check script, not the restaurant. The only coverage is a Yakima Herald "Cheap Eats" piece about its yakisoba (2018 URL). It's also mainly a burger place.
- Grab N Go Teriyaki, 1103 S 72nd Ave, Yakima: a Yakima Herald business-tidbits item (seen only as a search snippet; the page returned 429) said it was "opening soon" in the West Valley Pharmacy building. Needs proof that it opened.
- Jessie's Sushi & Teriyaki, 2304 S 1st St Suite A, Yakima: the listed URL is invalid (http://jessies.sushi.teriyaki/). No source found.
- Kobe Sushi and Teriyaki, 1905 S 1st St, Yakima: listed on the Kobe brand site (kobesri50.com, footer (c) 2019) with Oregon locations, and the phone matches. No Yakima menu and no 2025-26 source. It's a small chain and probably sushi-led. The Yakima Herald reported a separate new "Kobe Sushi and Ramen" at 6530 W Nob Hill Blvd.

## Not on the candidate list (found while searching)

- Inlander teriyaki roundup ("Hungry for teriyaki? Get your fill at these local spots, including two recent newcomers", inlander.com): returned 429, so I skipped it. Worth a later check for Spokane shops. The search snippet mentioned Kay's Teriyaki Plus (since 1996) and Teriyaki Spice.
- Ho Ho Teriyaki Chicken, Spokane: an Inlander listing exists, but status is unknown.
- Lylo's Original Teriyaki (Spokane): a food-stand vendor at Pig Out in the Park 2025 (Spokesman-Review, Aug 27, 2025), not a restaurant.
- Kinja Japanese and Korean, 7458 N Division St, Spokane: DoorDash storefront only. Probably not a teriyaki shop.
- Wok N' Roll Asian Express, 212 Fifth St, Wenatchee: has a chicken teriyaki plate (DoorDash storefront only). Unverified.
- Love Sushi, 1925 N Wenatchee Ave, Wenatchee: a Wenatchee World opening story (2023) reportedly says teriyaki is its most popular dish. Not opened or checked. It's a sushi place.
- Out of region: Ichi Teriyaki 19, 155 NE 192nd Ave #103, Camas (Clark County). Its own ordering site (ichiteriyaki19.com, (c) 2026) shows a full teriyaki menu. Pass this to the southwest/south Sound agent.
